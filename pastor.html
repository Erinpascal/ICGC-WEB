Pastor-in-Charge

Rev. Ato Kwamena Ghansah

Reverend Ato Kwamena Ghansah serves as the Lead Pastor of International Central Gospel Church — The Harvest Temple, Sunyani. Under his leadership, the church continues to grow as a vibrant community committed to raising leaders, shaping vision, and influencing society through Christ.

A passionate teacher of the Word, Rev. Ghansah is known for delivering inspiring messages that equip believers to excel in life and become practicing Christians. His ministry emphasizes spiritual depth, practical living, and helping people discover God's purpose for their lives.

Beyond the pulpit, Rev. Ghansah serves as faculty at the Central Leadership Program (CLP), where he trains the next generation of transformational leaders — leading sessions on deepening faith and faith-informed leadership.

The Harvest Temple operates under the spiritual oversight of Dr. Mensa Otabil, General Overseer of the International Central Gospel Church, and is part of the ICGC network serving the Bono Region of Ghana.

Too Precious To Lose

This book aims to help people identify, recover, and make the best use of the missing pieces of their lives. It guides the reader to evaluate themselves, identify the valuable pieces missing from their lives, understand why those pieces are missing, and know what to do when those pieces are found.

"I wrote this book in response to an identified need people have, in finding fulfillment and bliss in life, from a Christian perspective. Humanity is bound to the law of death — things don't get better, they get worse. We keep losing things all the time. However, I believe there are certain things which, regardless of the law of entropy at play, must not be lost. These things are simply too precious to lose."

— Rev. Ato Kwamena Ghansah

Key Themes

  • Identifying what is truly valuable in your life
  • Recovering the missing pieces that bring fulfillment
  • How monotony can rob you of great experiences
  • Learning more through observation than you ever imagined
  • Living with purpose from a Christian perspective
